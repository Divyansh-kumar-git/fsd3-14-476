Our Story & Passion

Brewing Happiness Since 2018

Welcome to Cafe Serenade, where every cup tells a story. What started as a small neighborhood coffee cart has grown into a vibrant space where coffee lovers, thinkers, and friends gather to share moments.

We believe that exceptional coffee starts at the source. That’s why we partner directly with ethical and sustainable coffee farms across Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala to bring you highest quality Arabica beans.
